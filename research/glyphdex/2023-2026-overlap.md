# Glyphdex vs our database, 2023-2026

Analysis date: 2026-10-25. Read-only: Glyphdex public API (`/api/documents`, `/api/documents/<id>` for sha256; ~270 detail calls at <=2.5 req/s) and a read-only SQL dump of `meetings`/`documents`/`organizations`.

Glyphdex has **156 meetings dated 2023-01-01 or later** (not ~60: 81 of them are Council). Each was compared with our meetings within +-1 day, and the SHA-256 of every Glyphdex PDF was compared with `documents.file_hash` (our `file_hash` is SHA-256 of our copy, so identical hashes prove the same file).

## How our database files these meetings (all confirmed by hash matches)

- **Committee of the Whole** is under organization `Council`, `meetings.type = 'Committee of the Whole'`, title "Committee of the Whole", archive folder `Committee of the Whole/`.
- **Council** is organization `Council`, type `Regular Council` or `Special Council`. Glyphdex's Regular/Special matches ours (kind-consistent in every hash match).
- **Community Advisory Committee** is our generic organization `Advisory Committee` (type `Advisory Committee`, title "Advisory Committee", archive folder `Joint Advisory Committee/`). The `Community Advisory Committee` organization row in our DB only holds one future planned meeting (2026-11-24).
- **Sustainable Development Advisory Committee** and **Board of Variance**: we have no past meetings. The SDAC organization row holds only a planned 2026-11-09 meeting. The eSCRIBE scraper never ingested SDAC (Mondays) or the Board of Variance.
- **Dates can differ by one day** between eSCRIBE and Glyphdex (e.g. CoW Glyphdex 2024-02-08 = ours 2024-02-09; Special Council Glyphdex 2024-12-16 1900 = ours 2024-12-17; CAC 2025-02-25 = ours 02-26). 22 of the 119 hash-confirmed duplicates are off by a day.
- Several Glyphdex rows can map to the same day (CoW + Special Council, or two Special Councils); the hash split them correctly in every case.

## Notable cases

- **7 Council duplicates without a hash match** (2023-03-20, 2024-05-06 1900, 2025-03-17, 2025-10-20, 2026-06-29, 2026-07-20, 2026-08-17): same date and kind as our meeting, but the bytes differ. Glyphdex holds a different build of the agenda package (e.g. 2025-10-20: 72 pages vs our 93; 2023-03-20: 168 vs 164+2) or only minutes while ours are eSCRIBE HTML/attachments (2026-07-20, 2026-08-17 have HTML agenda/minutes, no PDF). 2024-05-06 (242 pages) and 2025-03-17 (237 pages) have identical page counts to our agenda. All are the same meetings; importing them would duplicate.
- **SDAC 2025-03-10 / 2024-04-08 / 2025-01-21** sit on the same day (+-1) as one of our Special Council or CoW meetings. A naive date-only match would wrongly hide them, but those are different bodies (our meeting is a Council meeting with different PDFs), so they stay missing.
- **Board of Variance 2023-07-26** is next to our 2023-07-25 Advisory Committee, which Glyphdex's CAC 2023-07-25 matches by hash. Different body, so missing.
- **CAC 2023-07-11 (Special)**: our nearest Advisory Committee is 2023-07-25 (matched to Glyphdex's CAC 07-25 by hash). Genuinely missing.
- Reverse direction (ours but not in Glyphdex) is irrelevant for importing: e.g. 57 of our Council meetings, our 2023-04-11 CoW, and the 2026 Advisory Committee meetings (02-25, 03-31, 04-28, 05-26, 07-28) have no Glyphdex counterpart.
- Glyphdex minutes marked "(Draft)" exist for BoV 2023-07-26 and SDAC 2026-01-19; they would be imported as drafts.

## What was wrong with `already_ingested()`

The old rule (same date, `body in title or org == body`) only worked for Council (exact date, any non-"committee" title). It missed Committee of the Whole (title matches but "committee" excluded for Council, and the org is `Council`, not the body name), CAC (our org/title is `Advisory Committee`), and all one-day-off dates, so `--body "Committee of the Whole"` or CAC would have re-imported ~45 duplicates. It also ignored Regular vs Special (a Special that day hid a missing Regular).

New rule (`matches_existing()` in `apps/pipeline/pipeline/ingestion/glyphdex_import.py`): Council (kind-matched to type), Committee of the Whole and Community Advisory Committee match organization + type within +-1 day. Unmapped bodies (SDAC, Board of Variance, the 2016-2022 standing committees) keep the exact-date match on their own org/title, never a neighbouring Council meeting. With it, a dry run over 2023+ reports 126 already in our database and 30 to import, matching this analysis exactly. Residual risk: a genuinely missing CoW/CAC/Council meeting within one day of a same-type meeting of ours would be hidden (none in 2023-2026).

## Import command (dry run)

```bash
cd apps/pipeline
uv run python -m pipeline.ingestion.glyphdex_import \
  --body "Sustainable Development Advisory" --body "Board of Variance" --body "Community Advisory" \
  --from 2023-01-01 --dry-run
```

`--body` is now repeatable. Dropping `--dry-run` (add `--ingest`) would import exactly the 30 missing meetings (28 SDAC, 1 BoV, CAC 2023-07-11); the 20 CAC meetings we already have are skipped. Do not run it until the 2016-2022 backfill releases the pipeline lock. Note SDAC/BoV become new organizations named after the Glyphdex body, like the standing committees did.
