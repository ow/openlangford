# Langford 2008 / 2011 / 2014 election result sources

- 2008: CivicInfo BC (Wayback snapshot) http://web.archive.org/web/20201102000957/https://civicinfo.bc.ca/electionreports/candidates-and-results.php?year=2008

  - Mayor Stew Young acclaimed (CivicInfo flag). Dec 1 2008 inaugural minutes only receive the Proclamation, no numbers (GSPR doc eb4e6d59-cda0-4fe1-a3f0-db246d655c64).
- 2011: CivicInfo BC (Wayback snapshot) http://web.archive.org/web/20210811231451/https://www.civicinfo.bc.ca/electionreports/candidates-and-results.php?year=2011
  - Dec 5 2011 inaugural minutes (GSPR a279e791-e739-4387-891a-1ff14dab1e5d) contain no numbers.
- 2014: City proclamation + voting results, Chief Election Officer T. F. Moore, Nov 17 2014, in the Dec 1 2014 inaugural agenda package
  https://gspr.emc2.build/api/documents/5c6d5e90-b29a-4577-ac7c-6b1af081d394/artifacts/original (PDF pages 4-5)
- Turnout (all years, third-party, NOT official): https://votingvista.ca/canada/british-columbia/langford
