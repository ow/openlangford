Langford's last two regular council meetings of 2026 move a day later and an hour and a half earlier: to **5:30 pm on Tuesdays**, instead of **7:00 pm on Mondays**.

## How it was decided

The change was posted on the City's [meetings page](https://www.langford.ca/meetings/) as an order of the Mayor. It reads:

> By Order of the Mayor in accordance with Section 126 of the Community Charter the remainder of the Regular scheduled meetings of Council for 2026 shall now be held as follows: November 24, 2026 at 5:30pm, and December 8, 2026 at 5:30pm.

Council did not vote on it. It was not on the agenda of the inaugural meeting on November 2, the only council meeting since the election, and it does not appear in any other published agenda or minutes.

The order covers 2026 only. The 2027 meeting schedule has not been published yet.

## It has happened before

Council's start time has changed twice before, both times by a vote in an open meeting:

- **2008 to 2016:** meetings started at 7:00 pm.
- **August 2016:** Council voted to move the start to 5:30 pm. Stew Young was mayor.
- **November 2022:** the newly elected Council voted to go back to 7:00 pm from 2023.
- **November 2026:** Stew Young, mayor again, ordered the last two meetings of 2026 moved to 5:30 pm.

See [Council meeting start time](/matters/procedure-bylaw-amendment-change-meeting-start-time) for the earlier decisions.

## Why it matters

Regular council meetings are where Council makes most of its decisions and where residents can speak. A 5:30 pm start on a weekday is harder to reach for people who work until 5 or have a commute, school pickup or family dinner at that time.
